What this app stores

Your favourites, card boxes, best score and day streak are saved in your own browser storage on your device. They are never sent anywhere. Clearing website data or deleting the home-screen icon deletes them. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

What it downloads

The word list is downloaded once from this site and then kept on the device, which is why the app works with no connection. If word-list updates are switched on, the app checks for a newer list at most once every 90 days.
